Teach Yourself Sketchnotes

A sketchnote is a page that thinks in pictures. You mix a few words, a diagram, an arrow and a formula so your brain stores the idea twice — once as language, once as shape. This studio is the method. Computer Science XI and XII are the practice.

سکیچ نوٹ وہ صفحہ ہے جو تصویر میں سوچتا ہے۔ چند الفاظ، ایک خاکہ، ایک تیر اور ایک فارمولا — دماغ خیال کو دو بار محفوظ کرتا ہے۔

  • Use containers, connectors and stars as a visual vocabulary.
  • Lay notes with Flexbox: row, column, wrap and grow.
  • Hear Urdish — English terms inside Urdu explanation.
  • Keep formulas as MathJax, not as screenshots.
  • Zoom SVG diagrams without blur.
  • Study golden topics with the 1-3-7 day loop.

Visual vocabulary ★ Golden

Every page in this APK is a sketchnote. Four marks do almost all the work:

  • Container — a sticky box that holds one idea.
  • Connector — an arrow that shows sequence or cause.
  • Icon — a tiny picture (gate, stack, phone, person).
  • Emphasis — a star, underline or highlight for exam gold.

خانہ خیال کو باندھتا ہے، تیر بہاؤ دکھاتا ہے، آئیکن یاد دلاتا ہے، ستارہ بورڈ کے گولڈن ٹاپک کو چنتا ہے۔

1. What does a connector show?

2. When do you draw a star?

Answers: 1. Sequence or cause (flow). 2. On high-yield / golden exam topics.

Flexbox is how the page thinks ★ Golden

This whole teaching APK is laid out with CSS Flexbox. Think of your desk: notes sit in a row until they run out of space, then they wrap to the next line. A wide idea can grow. A sidebar can stay a column while the canvas stays a row.

Four words to draw in the margin:

  • flex-direction: row | column — the main axis.
  • flex-wrap: wrap — sticky notes overflow onto the next line.
  • gap — breathing space between cards.
  • flex: 1 1 240px — grow, shrink, basis.

فلیکس باکس صفحے کی سوچ ہے: قطار، کالم، لپیٹنا اور بڑھنا۔ یہی لے آؤٹ اس تدریسی ایپ کا انجن ہے۔

1. Which property lets cards jump to the next line on a phone?

2. What does flex: 1 1 240px mean in sketchnote language?

Answers: 1. flex-wrap. 2. Grow if there is space, shrink if needed, prefer about 240px as the starting width.

Urdish voice

Urdish is how a bilingual CS teacher actually talks: English for the term, Urdu for the meaning, in one breath.

Example spoken line:

Boolean algebra یعنی بولین الجبرا deals only with TRUE and FALSE یعنی درست اور غلط.

The APK splits that sentence by script. Latin letters go to an English voice. Nastaliq letters go to an Urdu voice. You hear one teacher, two engines.

اردش آواز انگریزی اصطلاح اور اردو وضاحت کو ایک لیکچر میں جوڑتی ہے۔ رسم الخط بدلتے ہی زبان بدل جاتی ہے۔

1. What triggers a voice-language switch?

Answers: 1. A change of writing script (Latin vs Arabic/Nastaliq).

MathJax formulas

Never photograph a formula. Typeset it. MathJax keeps the algebra selectable, zoomable and identical to the board-exam notation.

\[ Y = A \cdot B, \quad Y = A + B, \quad Y = A', \quad \text{rows} = 2^{n}, \quad O(n^{2}) \]

AND is 1 only when every input is 1:

\[ Y = A \cdot B \cdot C = 1 \iff A=B=C=1 \]

A truth table with four inputs has \(2^{4} = 16\) rows, not \(2 \times 4 = 8\).

فارمولا تصویر نہیں، اصلی ریاضی ہے۔ ٹروتھ ٹیبل کی قطاریں \(2^{n}\) ہوتی ہیں۔

1. How many rows for 5 inputs?

Answers: 1. \(2^{5} = 32\).

SVG diagrams

SVG is ink that can stretch. Logic-gate symbols, OSI stacks and neural nets stay sharp on a small phone and on a large tablet because they are vectors, not bitmaps.

ایس وی جی ویکٹر خاکہ ہے: زوم سے دھندلا نہیں ہوتا۔ لاجک گیٹ اور نیٹ ورک تہیں اسی لیے ایس وی جی میں ہیں۔

1. Why SVG instead of PNG for a logic gate?

Answers: 1. Vectors stay sharp at any size and stay small in the APK.

Board-exam sketchnote method

Use the same loop the booklets were written for:

  1. Preview the title and the objective list (5 minutes).
  2. Listen once in Urdish with the diagram on screen.
  3. Redraw the SVG on paper from memory.
  4. Check yourself before revealing answers.
  5. Spaced repeat after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days. Golden stars first.

Short answers earn marks with define + explain + example. Differentiate questions want a table. Logic and HCI questions want a labelled diagram. This app already drew the diagram — your job is to steal the structure onto the answer sheet.

عنوان دیکھو، اردش سنو، خاکہ کاغذ پر اتارو، سوال یاد سے حل کرو، ایک تین سات دن بعد دہراؤ۔

1. What three parts make a strong short answer?

Answers: 1. Definition, explanation, real-life example.
