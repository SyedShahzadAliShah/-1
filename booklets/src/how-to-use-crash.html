How to run this 30-Day Crash Course

This booklet is a complete 30-day exam plan. Days 1–23 teach every topic as revision cards. The last day of each chapter is a timed checkpoint. Days 24–26 are ★ Golden blitz. Day 27 is recaps. Day 28 is the mock. Day 29 you mark. Day 30 you repair and stop.

یہ تیس دن کا کریش کورس ہے۔ پہلے ۲۳ دن سب موضوعات، ہر باب کے آخر میں ٹیسٹ، آخری ہفتہ گولڈن، ماک پیپر اور اصلاح۔

Every teaching day — 3 hours, no phone

  1. 0–15 min. Recite yesterday's ★ Golden definition and redraw its table from memory.
  2. 15–105 min. Study today's cards. Copy every table. Say the Urdu line aloud.
  3. 105–135 min. Closed-book drill at the end of the day. Mark at once.
  4. 135–180 min. Homework: one 5-mark answer using Define · Explain · Example · Diagram · Working.

If you fall behind: do only the ★ Golden cards that day. Never skip a checkpoint day. Never start a new chapter until you can say that chapter's Golden definitions without looking.

The last seven days

  • Days 24–26. Golden blitz. No new non-Golden topics.
  • Day 27. Recaps only. Close the book and say each chapter aloud.
  • Day 28. Sit the mock in 2 hours 30 minutes. Hall conditions.
  • Day 29. Mark with the key. Write a repair list of every Golden you missed.
  • Day 30. Redo only that repair list. Sleep by 10 pm. Do not open a new topic.
