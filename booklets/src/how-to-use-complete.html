How to use this complete book

This is the one book for the whole grade. It combines every lesson, every ★ Golden topic, and every chapter exam in readable type. Each lesson starts on its own page and continues onto the next page when the topic needs more room. Nothing is shrunk to fit.

Three parts

  1. Part I — ★ Golden Topics. High-yield lessons only, full detail. Use this when time is short or in the last two weeks.
  2. Part II — Chapters. Every topic in syllabus order, including Golden ones. Learn one lesson at a time. Start each lesson on a fresh page.
  3. Part III — Exam preparation. A recap and a 75-mark closed-book paper for every chapter, then the final mock. Keys stay behind the seal.

How to sit a chapter exam

75 marks · 1 hour 15 minutes · no notes. Section A all 15 MCQ (1 mark). Section B all 10 short (3 marks). Section C any three long (10 marks); at least one must be ★ Golden. A 5-mark or 10-mark answer needs Define · Explain · Example · Diagram · Working.
