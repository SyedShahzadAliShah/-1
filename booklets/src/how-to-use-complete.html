How to use this complete book

This is the one book for the whole grade. It is chapter-wise lecture notes at readable type. ★ Golden concepts sit inside each chapter — they are not a separate book. Each lesson starts on its own page and continues at the same size when it needs more room. Blank pages are not used.

Inside every chapter

  1. Chapter opener. What you will learn, then the ★ Golden concepts of this chapter.

  3. The other lecture notes. Remaining topics in syllabus order.
  4. Exam preparation. Recap, then a 75-mark closed-book paper. The key is sealed.

How to sit a chapter exam

75 marks · 1 hour 15 minutes · no notes. Section A all 15 MCQ (1 mark). Section B all 10 short (3 marks). Section C any three long (10 marks); at least one must be ★ Golden. A 5-mark or 10-mark answer needs Define · Explain · Example · Diagram · Working.
