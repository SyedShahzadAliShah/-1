How to use this Study Guide

This is the official Self-Taught / Coaching-Academy Bootcamp combo for the whole grade. Students study alone. Teachers and academy coaches run the same pages as a bootcamp. ★ Golden concepts sit inside the chapter that teaches them. Each lesson starts on its own page and continues at the same type size. Blank pages are not used.

Two tracks — one book

Track A — Self-taught student. Read Learn it slowly. Cover the worked example and redo it on paper. Write Check yourself in your notebook. Do not open the chapter key. Sit the 75-mark chapter exam closed-book, then mark.

Track B — Coach / teacher. Academy: 90-minute batch (★ Golden = full class; other notes pair). School: 40-minute period (★ = one period). Use the Student · Coach strip on each lesson for board work and oral questions. Same exam, same sealed key.

Inside every chapter

  1. Chapter opener. Outcomes, ★ Golden concepts of this chapter, then the dual-track card (self-taught hours + academy classes).
  2. ★ Golden lecture notes first. High-yield ideas, full detail. Do these if time is short.
  3. The other lecture notes. Remaining topics in syllabus order.
  4. Exam preparation. Recap, then a 75-mark closed-book paper. The key is sealed for the student; the coach marks to the same scheme.

How to sit a chapter exam

75 marks · 1 hour 15 minutes · no notes. Section A all 15 MCQ (1 mark). Section B all 10 short (3 marks). Section C any three long (10 marks); at least one must be ★ Golden. A 5-mark or 10-mark answer needs Define · Explain · Example · Diagram · Working.
