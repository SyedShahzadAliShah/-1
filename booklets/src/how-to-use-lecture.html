How to use this BIEK Computer Science lecture

Each PDF is a classroom lecture from the BIEK / Sindh Computer Science textbooks (New Sindh Curriculum, STBB Grade XI–XII). Read Learn it, study the SVG diagram, try the worked example on paper, then answer Check yourself before looking at the answers. Extra board practice (MCQs, short and long questions) is added after the book content — it is not in the textbook. Career-only extras that were not in the book lecture list have been removed. Math is rendered with MathJax. ★ Golden topics are high-yield in board exams. The green box repeats the idea in Urdu.
