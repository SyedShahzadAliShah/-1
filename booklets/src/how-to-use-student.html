The 5-step self-study method

  1. Preview (5 minutes). Read "What you will learn" and turn each line into a question.
  2. Learn one topic. One topic fills one A4 page. Read Learn it, then reproduce the worked example on paper.
  3. Check yourself. Write answers in your notebook. Do not open the chapter Answer key yet.
  4. Sit the chapter exam. 75 marks, 1 hour 15 minutes, closed book (15 MCQ + 10 short + any 3 long). Then mark with the sealed key.
  5. Review. Tick the "I can…" list. Revisit gaps after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days.
