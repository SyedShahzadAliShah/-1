How to run this 30-Day Coaching Academy Edition

This book is the Bilingual Teacher’s Edition rebuilt as a 30-day coaching academy batch. It is not a 40-minute school period and not a 3-hour self-study crash. A standard academy class is 90 minutes. The coach stands at the board. Students copy, drill, and take homework home. ★ Golden topics are never skipped.

The 30 days

  • Days 1–23 — Teach the whole grade. Every topic from the teacher’s notes appears as a board card. The last day of each chapter is a 75-mark closed-book exam in the academy hall.
  • Days 24–26 — ★ Golden blitz. No new non-Golden topic. Error clinic on high-yield board wording.
  • Day 27 — Oral recap. Students say each chapter recap at the board, books closed.
  • Day 28 — Mock morning. 2 hours 30 minutes. Hall conditions. No notes, no phones.
  • Day 29 — Mark and repair list. Mark to the sealed key. Every missed Golden goes on the list.
  • Day 30 — Repair clinic, then stop. Redo only the list. Dismiss by 6 pm.

The 90-minute academy class (Days 1–23 teach days)

  1. 0–10 min — Starter test. Three oral or written 1-mark questions from yesterday. Marks go in the batch register in this book.
  2. 10–35 min — Concept lock. Write the definition, table and diagram on the whiteboard. Students copy. Phones in the box at the door.
  3. 35–55 min — Board working. Do the worked example. Hide it. Two students redo it on the board.
  4. 55–75 min — Academy drill. Timed. 1-mark, then 3-mark, then 5-mark. Golden days also take an 8-mark. Mark at once with the recipe.
  5. 75–85 min — Error clinic. Collect the three most common wrong answers. Correct them in one box on the board.
  6. 85–90 min — Homework out. One 5-mark (or 8-mark if Golden) + redraw the board from memory. Collect next class. Stamp full / half / zero.

Chapter-exam days

On the last day of a chapter the class is a hall paper: 75 marks · 1 hour 15 minutes · 15 MCQ + 10 short + any 3 long. Use the leftover minutes to collect scripts. Do not mark in front of the batch. Re-teach any Golden below 60% in the next starter.

How academy answers score marks

  • 1 mark: one exact definition or one named fact.
  • 2 marks: definition + one local example (JazzCash, school portal, NADRA, load-shedding).
  • 3 marks: definition + two clear points + example.
  • 5 marks: definition, a four-point table or explanation, example, and a labelled diagram if the topic has one.
  • 8–10 marks: 5-mark structure + full working (trace, K-map, code, ER diagram) + a one-line conclusion.

Train this wording every class: Define. Explain. Example. Diagram. Working. An answer with no working is a half-answer.

Batch rules

  • One 90-minute class a day, six days a week, for thirty days. No extra unpaid “make-up” class unless a Golden exam mark is below 60%.
  • If the batch falls behind, drop non-Golden cards that day. Never skip a chapter exam, the mock, or a Golden blitz day.
  • Homework is collected. Uncollected homework is a zero in the register, not a warning.
