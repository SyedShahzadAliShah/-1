How to use this Self-Paced Edition

This volume is the published all-in-one for a student who studies alone. Every topic is a numbered lesson with a time, a “Do this now” line, and a gate before the next lesson. Check yourself answers sit at the end of each chapter, after a seal page. It is not the Teacher’s Edition, the Academy batch book, or the 30-day crash course.

The lesson loop

  1. One lesson, one page. Each topic is printed on a single A4 leaf. Do not turn over until the gate says so.
  2. Read the lesson head. Note the number, the minutes, and whether it is ★ Golden.
  3. Learn it. Read the explanation once slowly. Turn each heading into a question.
  4. Example on paper. Cover the worked example. Reproduce it. Then uncover and mark.
  5. Check yourself. Write answers in your notebook. Do not open the chapter Answer key.
  6. Gate. If a check was wrong, re-read that lesson. If it was clean, go on.

At the end of the chapter, sit the practice paper in exam conditions. Only then break the seal and mark.

Three published paces

  • Steady — 16 weeks. The default. Two to three lessons a weekday. Chapter paper on the weekend.
  • Term — 8 weeks. One chapter a week. Golden lessons in full; non-Golden checks as same-day review.
  • Exam — 4 weeks. ★ Golden lessons first. Then one chapter paper every two days. Mock in the last week.

Pick one pace on the Study planner page and stay on it. Do not mix tracks in the same week.
