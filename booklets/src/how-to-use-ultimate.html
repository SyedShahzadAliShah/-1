How to use this Ultimate Edition

This is the published Teach Yourself volume for the whole grade. Every topic is a numbered lesson at readable type. Formulae are set with MathJax as SVG. Diagrams are inline SVG. Side-by-side layouts use Flexbox. Answers stay sealed after each chapter exam so you practise first.

The lesson loop

  1. Read the lesson head. Number, minutes, and whether it is ★ Golden.
  2. Learn it. Read once slowly. Turn each heading into a question.
  3. Example on paper. Cover the worked example. Reproduce it. Then uncover and mark.
  4. Diagram / formula. Copy every labelled SVG and every typeset formula into your notebook.
  5. Check yourself. Write answers in your notebook. Do not open the chapter key.
  6. Gate. If a check was wrong, re-read. If it was clean, go on.

At the end of the chapter, read the recap, then sit the Chapter Exam (75 marks, 1 hour 15 minutes, closed book). Only then break the seal and mark.

Three published paces

Steady — 16 weeks. The published default. Two or three lessons a weekday. Chapter paper on the weekend.

Term — 8 weeks is one chapter a week. Exam — 4 weeks is ★ Golden first, then papers, then the mock.

Pick one pace on the Study planner and stay on it. Do not mix tracks in the same week.
