# Final Mini-Essay — Session 17

**Time:** 8 minutes plan + 40 minutes write + 7 minutes proofread  
**Length:** 250–300 words  
**Marks:** 40 (language 25 + structure 15)

---

## Prompt

**A state that cannot tax fairly cannot spend honestly.**

Take a position. British spelling. No contractions. Name at least two institutions.

---

## Language rubric /25

| Band | Marks | What the script looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean | 21–25 | Fewer than 3 grammar faults per 100 words. Tense controlled. Articles and prepositions mostly right. No cliché stack. Voice on purpose. |
| Workable | 16–20 | Meaning always clear. Recurring but limited faults (e.g. articles). No collapse of sentence integrity. |
| Fragile | 10–15 | Frequent SVA/tense/Urduism. Some splices or fragments. Examiner would get tired. |
| Not ready | 0–9 | Language hides the argument. Run-ons, tense soup, empty passives, cliché open. |

Deduct inside the band for: comma splices, *if…will*, *unless…not*, *I am agree*, empty *steps should be taken*, *the need of the hour*, dictionary opening.

---

## Structure rubric /15

| Beat | Marks | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thesis that unpacks both **tax** and **spend** | 4 | Last sentence of opening is a claim, not a heading |
| Body 1 — fairness of tax | 3 | Institution named; PEEL-ish |
| Body 2 — honesty of spend | 3 | Linked to body 1, not a new essay |
| Close — named actors + modal | 3 | *must/should*, no slogan |
| Cohesion | 2 | *this + noun* or old-to-new; not *moreover* four times |

---

## Partner codes (margin)

**T** tense · **A** article/agreement · **P** preposition/punctuation/pair · **S** structure (fragment, splice, voice, modifier, parallelism)

Star **one gold sentence**. Bracket **one wreck**.
