# Session 6 — Tenses I: The Present System

**Module B · Time, voice, narration**  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Exam link:** tense items; essay claims about “what is true now”; Precis present-tense summary habit

---

## Outcomes

The student can:

1. Form and name all four **present** tenses.
2. Choose present simple for general CSS claims, present perfect for a past that still matters.
3. Pair time markers with the right present form (*every year, now, since, for, already, yet*).
4. Keep a paragraph in the present system without slipping into random past.
5. Avoid *since five years* and present perfect + *yesterday*.

## Student notes

### 1. Why tense is an essay skill

Tense is not a school chart. It tells the examiner **when** your claim lives.

- A thesis is usually **present simple**: *Unequal taxation undermines federalism.*  
- A continuing crisis is often **present perfect**: *Pakistan has mismanaged water for fifty years.*  
- A scene happening around us can be **present continuous**: *Cities are sinking their own water tables.*

If you hop from *undermines* to *undermined* to *will undermining* without a reason, the essay looks uneducated even when the ideas are good.

### 2. The four present forms

Let the verb be *fail* (regular) and *go* (irregular).

| Form | Pattern | Time markers | CSS job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Present simple** | *fail(s)* | *always, usually, every year, in general* | laws of politics, definitions, thesis, habits of institutions |
| **Present continuous** | *am/is/are + -ing* | *now, currently, this year, still* | temporary or developing situation |
| **Present perfect** | *has/have + past participle* | *already, yet, just, ever, never, **since** + point, **for** + period, so far* | past with a present result; unfinished time (*this decade*) |
| **Present perfect continuous** | *has/have been + -ing* | *since, for, all day, lately* | duration of an activity still going on |

Examples:

- Simple: *The Constitution **guarantees** dignity.*  
- Continuous: *The cabinet **is bargaining** over the NFC award.*  
- Perfect: *The cabinet **has postponed** the census twice.*  
- Perfect continuous: *Households **have been rationing** power all summer.*

### 3. Present simple: the CSS default

Use it for:

1. thesis and topic sentences  
2. facts treated as generally true  
3. newspaper-style comment on institutions  
4. plot of a precis (the original may be past; a precis often stays present — your precis teacher will confirm)

Form:

- I/you/we/they **fail**  
- he/she/it / singular noun **fails**  
- negative: *does not fail / do not fail*  
- question: *Does the state fail the poor?*

### 4. Stative verbs: do not put them in continuous

These describe a state, not an action: *know, believe, understand, own, seem, need, consist, contain, prefer, agree, mean, remember, belong*.

- *The file **contains** evidence.* not *is containing*  
- *I **agree**.* not *I am agreeing* (except “I am agreeing with you on this one point today” — too spoken for CSS)

*think*: *I think the policy is weak* (opinion = simple). *The committee is thinking the problem through* (activity = continuous).

### 5. Present perfect vs past simple (the big fight)

| Present perfect | Past simple (Session 7) |
| --- | --- |
| no finished time, or time still open | finished time named |
| *The state has raised the policy rate.* | *The state raised the policy rate in June 2023.* |
| *Have you read the report?* | *Did you read the report yesterday?* |

**Illegal:** *The state has raised the rate yesterday.*  
**Illegal:** *Pakistan is independent since 1947.* → *Pakistan **has been** independent **since** 1947.* / *Pakistan **became** independent **in** 1947.*

### 6. since / for / ago

- **since** + starting point: *since 2018, since the 18th Amendment, since Monday*  
- **for** + length: *for six years, for decades*  
- **ago** belongs to **past simple**, not present perfect: *six years ago the Act was passed.*

### 7. Irregular participles you will need

*be — was/were — been*  
*do — did — done*  
*go — went — gone*  
*write — wrote — written*  
*rise — rose — risen* (prices rise; the state *raises* taxes)  
*raise — raised — raised*  
*lie — lay — lain* (to rest) vs *lay — laid — laid* (to put)  
*seek — sought — sought*  
*show — showed — shown*

*Inflation has **risen**. The SBP has **raised** the rate.*

## Urdu-interference box

| Don’t | Do |
| --- | --- |
| *He is knowing the law.* | *He knows the law.* |
| *I am living in Lahore since 2019.* | *I have been living / have lived in Lahore since 2019.* |
| *She has gone yesterday.* | *She went yesterday.* |
| *Since five years the policy failed.* | *For five years the policy has failed.* / *The policy failed five years ago.* |
| *The sun is rising in the east.* (as a law) | *The sun rises in the east.* |

## Class drill

**A. Name the tense and say why it is used.**

1. The rupee depreciates when reserves fall.  
2. The rupee is depreciating this quarter.  
3. The rupee has depreciated by a third.  
4. The rupee has been depreciating since the flood year.

**B. Choose**

5. Pakistan (is / has been) a federation since 1973.  
6. The court (delivers / is delivering / has delivered) the verdict an hour ago.  
7. She (knows / is knowing) the file.  
8. (Since / For / Ago) 2010, urban flooding (became / has become) normal in Karachi.  
9. The committee (has not yet publish / has not yet published / did not yet published) the report.  
10. Water tables (fall / are falling / have fallen) every year — pick the best for a **general law**, then the best for **this decade’s trend**. Write both sentences.

**C. Repair**

11. The government has announced the package last Monday.  
12. He has wrote a strong outline.  
13. Inflation is increased.  
14. I am agree that the present simple fits a thesis.

## Essay-transfer task

Topic: **Corruption weakens the state.**

Write **140–160 words**.

Rules:

- Use **only** the present system (the four forms above).  
- At least one present simple thesis.  
- At least one present perfect with *since* or *for*.  
- At least one present continuous for a current process.  
- Circle every finite verb and write the tense in the margin.  
- No *yesterday, in 1999, after the war* — those force the past.

Peer audit: if a partner finds a past simple, it is a fault unless you can justify a quotation.

## Exit ticket

1. Which tense for a CSS thesis?  
2. *since 2018* pairs with which present tense family?  
3. Correct: *She has submitted the form yesterday.*  
4. Why is *is knowing* wrong?

## Teacher mark pointers

- 6: *delivered* + *an hour ago* = past simple, not present perfect.  
- 8: *Since 2010 … has become*.  
- 13: *Inflation has increased* / *is increasing* (not *is increased* unless passive with agent: *is increased by seasonal demand* — awkward). Prefer *has increased*.  
- In the transfer, do not punish a student who needs *must* (modal); modals are fine. Punish *was / were / did*.
