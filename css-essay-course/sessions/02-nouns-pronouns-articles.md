# Session 2 — Parts of Speech I: Nouns, Pronouns, Articles

**Module A · Foundation**  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Exam link:** Grammar & vocabulary (articles); sentence correction; every CSS paragraph

---

## Outcomes

The student can:

1. Sort nouns into countable, uncountable, and collective.
2. Use *one of the + plural noun + singular verb*.
3. Keep a pronoun’s **antecedent** clear and in agreement.
4. Choose **a / an / the / zero article** in CSS topics (the state, poverty, the Constitution, education).
5. Repair the ten article errors that FPSC recycles.

## Student notes

### 1. Why parts of speech come first

If you cannot name the job of a word, you cannot repair it. CSS sentence correction is diagnosis, not guesswork. We start with the naming words.

### 2. Nouns

A **noun** names a person, place, thing, idea, or event.

| Kind | Test | CSS examples | Fault |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Countable | you can number them | *a reform, two courts, many voters* | *many evidence* |
| Uncountable | no *a/an*, no plural *-s* | *information, advice, furniture, machinery, progress, poverty, inflation* | *informations, advices, furnitures* |
| Collective | group as one unit | *government, committee, cabinet, police* | verb number: BrE *the committee has*; *the police are* |
| Proper | named, capitalised | *Pakistan, the Indus, Parliament, the Constitution* | *pakistan*, *the parliament* (in CSS, *Parliament* when meaning the institution) |

**Quantity words**

- Countable: *many, few, a few, several, a number of, each*  
- Uncountable: *much, little, a little, an amount of*  
- Both: *some, any, a lot of, plenty of*

*A number of reforms **are** overdue.* (plural)  
*The number of reforms **is** small.* (singular)

### 3. The deadly pattern: *one of the*

Formula: **one of the + plural noun + singular verb**

- *One of the **reasons** **is** elite capture.*  
- *One of the **problems** that **face** the state **is** water theft.*  
  (*that face* agrees with *problems*; *is* agrees with *one*.)

Never: *one of the reason is*.

### 4. Pronouns

A pronoun stands for a noun already clear to the reader (the **antecedent**).

Rules:

1. **Agreement:** *Pakistan must reform **its** tax base.* (country = *it/its*, not *their*, unless you are personifying in informal talk — don’t.)
2. **Each / every / everyone / nobody** take *he or she* in old style, or recast in the plural: *Citizens must pay **their** taxes.* Recasting is cleaner.
3. **Who / whom:** *who* = subject; *whom* = object. *The officer **who** signed*; *the officer **whom** the court summoned*. If in doubt in an essay, recast: *the officer the court summoned*.
4. **Which / that:** *which* often adds extra information (commas); *that* defines. *The bill **that** failed in the Senate…*
5. **Vague *this/it*:** never start a paragraph with *This is very important* unless *this* points to a named noun. Write *This delay…* / *This gap in data…*

### 5. Articles — the CSS minefield

English has **a / an / the / nothing (zero)**. Urdu has no matching system. This is why article errors are the loudest “non-native” signal in a CSS script.

#### a / an (indefinite)

Use for one of a class, first mention, or a job.

- *A state cannot survive without tax.*  
- *She became **an** officer.*  
- *an hour, an honest effort, an MBA, an FPSC paper* (go by **sound**)  
- *a university, a European state, a one-time grant* (sound of *yoo / y / w*)

#### the (definite)

Use when the reader can identify which one:

- already mentioned: *A policy was launched. **The** policy failed.*  
- unique: *the sun, the Constitution, the Prime Minister, the Indus, the poor*  
- superlative / ordinal: *the worst crisis, the first census*  
- known institutions: *the Supreme Court, the State Bank, the United Nations*  
- rivers, mountain ranges, oceans, newspapers: *the Himalayas, the Indian Ocean, the Dawn* (paper often styled *Dawn*)

#### zero article

No article for:

- general uncountable or general plural: *Poverty breeds unrest. Courts need independence.*  
- most country names: *Pakistan, Turkey, China* (but *the United States, the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, the Punjab* in some styles — in CSS write *Punjab* or *the Punjab* consistently; this course uses *Punjab* for the province and *the Punjab* only if you mean the historic region. Prefer *Punjab*.)  
- languages, sports, academic subjects: *Urdu, English, hockey, economics*  
- meals, in general: *after breakfast*

**Good CSS pair**

- *Education is a public good.* (zero: education in general)  
- *The education budget is still too small.* (a specific budget)

### 6. Capital letters

Capitalise proper nouns, not big ideas. *democracy, inflation, climate change* stay lower case unless they start the sentence. *Islam, Christianity, Parliament, Article 19* take capitals as names.

## Class drill

**A. Article cloze** (*a / an / the / —* )

1. ___ rule of law is not ___ slogan; it is ___ daily habit of institutions.  
2. ___ information that ___ bureau released was incomplete.  
3. ___ honest broker is rare in ___ regional politics.  
4. ___ poverty in ___ Thar is not ___ same as poverty in a city slum.  
5. ___ United Nations warned about ___ water stress in ___ Indus basin.

**B. Repair**

6. One of the main cause of unrest are unemployment.  
7. Pakistan must protect their farmers.  
8. This is alarming. (Make *this* specific, using *census delay*.)  
9. She is a honest civil servant.  
10. Many advices were ignored.

## Essay-transfer task

Write **120 words** on: **Poverty is a threat to national security.**

Constraints:

- At least two uncountable nouns used correctly (*poverty, security, information, inflation, evidence*).  
- One sentence with *one of the…*  
- Circle every article. If a noun has no article, write **Ø** above it.

## Exit ticket

1. Why is *informations* wrong?  
2. Fill: *One of the ___ (reform) ___ (be) delayed.*  
3. *a* or *an*: ___ FPSC examiner; ___ unique case; ___ hour.  
4. Correct: *Pakistan must reform their civil service.*

## Teacher mark pointers

- Item A1: *The* rule of law; *a* slogan; *a* daily habit.  
- A4: zero *poverty* (general) then *the Thar*; *the same*.  
- Do not accept *the Pakistan*.  
- *The Parliament* is commonly used; prefer *Parliament* as institution, *the Parliament* if meaning this sitting. Accept both if consistent.  
- *Poor needs the state* → *The poor need the state.*
