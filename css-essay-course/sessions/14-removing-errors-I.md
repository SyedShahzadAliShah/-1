# Session 14 — Removing Errors I (FPSC Families)

**Module C · Accuracy and transfer**  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Exam link:** Precis paper sentence correction (10 marks) + silent essay language marks

---

## Outcomes

The student can:

1. Name the FPSC instruction: **smallest needed correction only**.
2. Diagnose five families: **agreement, articles, prepositions, tense/time, unless-until-if**.
3. Rewrite a faulty sentence without recasting it into a different essay.
4. Explain the repair in one rule-phrase.
5. Start a personal “top 20 faults” list from the error log.

---

## Teacher plan (90 minutes)

| Time | Move |
| --- | --- |
| 0–8 | Recap comma splice vs *however*. |
| 8–20 | Teach the method (below). Put the five families on the wall. |
| 20–55 | 15 timed items. 90 seconds each. Students write *Family + corrected sentence*. Then pair-check against the teacher’s key. |
| 55–80 | Transfer: a 120-word dirty paragraph containing **planted** errors from the five families. Students correct in situ (do not rewrite from scratch). |
| 80–90 | Homework, exit. Build top-20 list. |

**Traps:** students “improve” style and lose the exam mark; two corrections when one was asked; changing meaning.

**Stretch:** items with two legal repairs — discuss which is *smallest*.

---

## Student notes

### 1. The official skill

FPSC’s sentence-correction instruction (Precis & Composition) is the law of this room:

> Rewrite with really needed correction only, without making unnecessary alterations.

That is also how you should proofread a CSS essay in the last ten minutes: **hunt the fault, change the smallest piece, leave the rest**.

### 2. The method (90 seconds an item)

1. Read once for meaning.  
2. Test the **five families** in order (they cover most papers):  
   - **A** agreement (subject–verb, one of, many a, neither/nor)  
   - **Art** articles and countability  
   - **P** preposition / pair (*prefer to, despite, discuss*)  
   - **T** tense and time markers  
   - **C** condition (*if will, unless not, until not*)  
3. Make the smallest change.  
4. Re-read. If a new fault appeared, you recast too much.  
5. Write the **family name** in the margin. If you cannot name it, you guessed.

Later (Session 15) we add parallelism, modifiers, diction, Urduisms.

### 3. Family A — agreement (reminders)

- *The quality of X **is***  
- *One of the **reasons is***  
- *Many a **student fails***  
- *Each of the files **has***  
- *Neither the notes nor the **outline is*** / *Neither the outline nor the **notes are***  
- *He is one of the officers who **have***  
- *The poor **are***

### 4. Family Art — articles and count

- *an honest, a university, an FPSC paper*  
- *information, advice, evidence* — uncountable  
- *the Constitution, Pakistan* (no *the*), *the poor*  
- *one of the **most serious problems***

### 5. Family P — small words

- *discuss Ø, comprise Ø, consist of, despite Ø, in spite of*  
- *prefer to, senior to, different from, interested in, accused of, charged with*  
- *since* + point; *for* + period  
- *between* two; *among* a group

### 6. Family T — tense and time

- no present perfect + *yesterday / last year / in 2010*  
- *did not* + base  
- *has written*, not *has wrote*  
- thesis in present; dated fact in past  
- *since 2018* + present perfect

### 7. Family C — condition

- *If* + present, *will* in the other clause  
- *unless* without extra *not*  
- *until* + the event you wait for (positive)  
- *If I were* / *If she had known* (not *would have* in the if-clause)

### 8. What “smallest change” looks like

Faulty: *One of the main reason are unemployment.*  
Over-rewrite: *Unemployment is a major driver of unrest among urban youth.* (new sentence — **zero marks** if this were a correction item)  
Smallest: *One of the main **reasons is** unemployment.*

## Urdu-interference box (preview; full hunt in Session 15)

Even in these five families, Urdu leaves fingerprints: *one of the reason, discuss about, since five years, if I will, unless you don’t.* Diagnose in English rule-names, not “it sounds wrong.”

## Class drill (timed)

For each: **Family** + **corrected sentence**. Do not recast.

1. The quality of these reforms are poor.  
2. She discussed about the NFC award.  
3. He has submitted the form yesterday.  
4. One of the most serious problem are extremism.  
5. If the monsoon will fail, wheat will be imported.  
6. Despite of the stay, demolition began.  
7. Many a candidates have failed the essay.  
8. Pakistan is facing this crisis since 2018.  
9. The cabinet comprises of thirty ministers.  
10. Wait until the House does not rise.  
11. Neither the minister nor the aides was briefed. (aides = nearer)  
12. She did not wrote the précis.  
13. An university degree is not an employment.  
14. Unless you don’t meter the wells, the city will go dry.  
15. He is one of those officers who signs every file.

---

## Essay-transfer task

Dirty paragraph (correct **in situ**; do not rewrite from a blank page):

*Poverty is one of the main threat to the national security, government is not serious about it since many years. If the state will not tax the elite the poor will keep paying a sales tax. Despite of the speeches, informations on the true budget is weak. Many a programmes has been launched, however they collapses after the first year. The quality of these programmes are not audited. Unless the cabinet does not publish the figures, the debate will remain childish.*

Count your corrections. Aim for **every** planted fault, nothing else.

---

## Homework

1. Repeat the 15 class items from memory the next morning.  
2. From your error log, pick 10 personal faults and write a “smallest change” repair plus family name.  
3. Write 5 new faulty sentences (deliberately) for a partner to diagnose — then key them.  
4. Read Session 15’s family list once.

---

## Exit ticket

1. What does FPSC forbid besides leaving the error?  
2. Name the five families of this session.  
3. Smallest repair: *She has met him last Monday.*  
4. Why is *Unemployment is rising among the youth* a bad “correction” of item 4?

---

## Teacher mark pointers

- 11: nearer subject *aides* → *were*.  
- 13: *A university degree is not employment.* (or *not a guarantee of employment* — slightly bigger; accept *not employment* / *not a job*).  
- 14: drop *don’t*: *Unless you meter the wells…*  
- 15: *who **sign*** (officers).  
- Dirty paragraph key is in the answer-key file. If a student also “improves” *childish* to *uninformed*, do not punish, but remind them that the exam item would not require it.
