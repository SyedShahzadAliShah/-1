# Session 15 — Removing Errors II (Parallelism, Modifiers, Diction, Urduisms)

**Module C · Accuracy and transfer**  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Exam link:** remaining correction types; the language that makes a CSS essay look “Pakistani-translated”

---

## Outcomes

The student can:

1. Repair **parallelism** after *and, or, not only…but also, both…and*.
2. Place **modifiers** next to the word they describe; fix dangling *-ing*.
3. Replace **wrong words** (*affect/effect, principal/principle, lose/loose*) in a CSS sentence.
4. Translate-back the **Urduism list** into examiner-safe English.
5. Clean a full student-style paragraph of mixed faults, still using smallest change where possible, then a second pass for diction.

---

## Teacher plan (90 minutes)

| Time | Move |

**Traps:** over-Latinising (*henceforth, wherein, aforementioned*); replacing Urduisms with equally empty “academy English”.

**Stretch:** misplaced only: *The committee only published the summary in June* vs *only the summary* vs *only in June*.

## Student notes

### 1. Parallelism

Equal ideas take equal form.

| Stem | Parallel |
| --- | --- |
| *and / or* | *to tax, to spend and to explain* |
| *not only … but also* | *not only **corrupt** but also **incompetent*** (adj + adj) |
| *both … and* | *both **in law** and **in practice*** |
| *either … or* | *either **publish** or **resign*** |
| comparisons | *better **to audit** than **to guess*** |

Faulty: *The job is to examine files, interviewing witnesses and a report.*  
Repaired: *The job is to examine files, interview witnesses and write a report.*

### 2. Modifiers must sit next to the thing they describe

**Dangling:** the opening phrase has no doer in the subject that follows.

- Faulty: *Walking into the secretariat, the files were on the floor.* (the files were not walking)  
- Right: *Walking into the secretariat, **I** found the files on the floor.*  
- Right: *When I walked into the secretariat, the files were on the floor.*

**Misplaced:**

- Faulty: *The minister said in June the bill would die in the House.* (did he say it in June, or would it die in June?)  
- Right: *In June the minister said that the bill would die in the House.*  
  or *The minister said that the bill would die in the House in June.*

**only, almost, even** go next to the word they limit.

### 3. Diction: words CSS students confuse

Use the meaning, then a CSS sentence.

| Pair | Meaning | CSS line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| affect / effect | v. influence / n. result (also v. to bring about) | *Inflation **affects** the poor. The **effect** is hunger.* |
| principal / principle | chief / moral rule | *the **principal** officer; a **principle** of justice* |
| advice / advise | noun / verb | *advice is cheap; I **advise** silence* |
| practice / practise | n. / v. (BrE) | *in **practice**; to **practise** law* |
| licence / license | n. / v. (BrE) | *a driving **licence**; to **license** a channel* |
| lose / loose | fail to keep / not tight | *lose an election; a **loose** coalition* |
| compliment / complement | praise / complete | *the two policies **complement** each other* |
| council / counsel | body / advice | *the cabinet is not a **council** of war; **counsel** of caution* |
| stationary / stationery | unmoving / paper | *prices were not **stationary**; buy **stationery*** |
| economic / economical | of the economy / thrifty | *economic policy; an **economical** stove* |
| historic / historical | important in history / about history | *a **historic** vote; **historical** evidence* |
| political / politic | of politics / prudent | *a **politic** silence* |
| imply / infer | speaker suggests / listener concludes | *The report **implies** fraud. We **infer** that the audit failed.* |
| fewer / less | count / uncount | *fewer jobs, less inflation* |
| between / among | two / group | |

Ten pairs is enough for this session. A full pair-of-words bank belongs to the Precis course.

### 5. Two-pass proofreading in the exam hall

**Pass 1 (grammar):** families from Sessions 14–15. Circle verbs, articles, prepositions.  
**Pass 2 (diction):** kill clichés and wrong pairs.  
**Pass 3 (only if time):** sentence length. Split anything you cannot read in one breath.

Never start Pass 2 before Pass 1. A pretty cliché that disagrees with its subject still fails.

## Urdu-interference box (word order)

Urdu *SOV* (*wo file complete kar chuka hai*) can produce:

- *He the file has completed yesterday.* → *He completed the file yesterday.*  
- *Is policy of the government* as a stacked *of*: *The government’s policy* is tighter.

## Class drill

**A. Parallelism and modifiers**

1. The act requires the board to audit accounts, publishing names and a fine.  
2. Not only the FBR but also wasting time is the problem. (make a legal not only…but also)  
3. Rushing to the House, the bill was tabled.  
4. The committee only met the unions in March. (three different meanings — write all three)

**B. Diction**

5. The new tax will effect retailers.  
6. She lost a lose coalition.  
7. We infer in the first paragraph that the writer hates the poor; the writer actually implies a subsidy. (repair the pair)  
8. There are less jobs than applicants.

**C. Urduisms / clichés**

9. According to me, government should take concrete steps.  
10. Last but not the least, we must discuss about education.  
11. Although the policy is sound but implementation is weak.  
12. I am agree that informations are missing.

## Essay-transfer task

Dirty body paragraph:

*In this contemporary era the youth of Pakistan is facing a lot of problems, unemployment is one of the main reason. Walking into the cities, jobs are not there. Government should take concrete steps, provide skill and that the industry is growing. According to me if the state will not listen the youth will go towards frustration. Last but not the least, more better education is the need of the hour and it will effect the future of the masses.*

**Pass 1:** grammar (agreement, tense, articles, prepositions, conditionals, voice, modifiers, parallelism, punctuation).  
**Pass 2:** diction and clichés.  
Write the cleaned paragraph in **110–140 words**. Keep the meaning. Do not add a fake quotation.

## Exit ticket

1. Repair the dangling: *Reading the report, the errors were obvious.*  
2. *affect* or *effect*: Inflation will ___ the poor. The ___ will be hunger.  
3. Two replacements for *take concrete steps*.  
4. Why is *not only corrupt but also a failure* ugly?

---

## Teacher mark pointers

- 2 possible: *Not only the FBR but also the courts wasted time.* / *The problem is not only delay at the FBR but also wasted hearings.*  
- 3: *Rushing to the House, **the minister** tabled the bill.*  
- 4: *met the unions only in March* / *met only the unions in March* / *only the committee met the unions in March*.  
- 7: *The first paragraph **implies**…; we **infer**…*  
- Cleaned transfer should not still contain *need of the hour* or *masses* if Pass 2 was done. A student who keeps one cliché but has perfect grammar: mark diction, not grammar.
