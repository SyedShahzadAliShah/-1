[y=94 x=57 R12.0] This case study demonstrates the step-by-step development of an ER schema,
[y=112 x=57 R12.0] from requirement analysis to the final ER diagram for a college library.
[y=145 x=57 B14.4] Step 1: Requirement Analysis
[y=174 x=57 R10.8] What does the system need to do?
[y=189 x=57 R10.8] - A book has an ID, title, author, and publisher.
[y=206 x=57 R10.8] - A member (student/teacher) has an ID, name, and contact info.
[y=222 x=57 R10.8] - A member can borrow multiple books; a book can be borrowed by different members
[y=238 x=57 R10.8] over time.
[y=255 x=57 R10.8] - The system must record Issue Date and Return Date.
