[y=54 x=57 B19.2] Referential Integrity Operations
[y=94 x=57 R12.0] To maintain consistency automatically without manual effort, DBMS provides
[y=112 x=57 R12.0] two powerful operations.
[y=146 x=65 B14.4] 1. Cascade Update
[y=175 x=65 R12.0] If a Primary Key value is changed in the parent table, that exact change is
[y=193 x=65 B12.0] automatically updated in all related child tables.
[y=219 x=64 R10.2] Example: If Ali's StudentID changes from 101 to 201 in the Student table, his ID will
[y=234 x=65 R10.2] automatically change to 201 in the Enrollments table. No data is lost or disconnected.
[y=285 x=65 B14.4] 2. Cascade Delete
[y=313 x=65 R12.0] If a record is deleted from the parent table, all related records in child tables
[y=331 x=65 B12.0] are also deleted automatically.
[y=357 x=64 R10.2] Example: If student Sara (ID 102) leaves the school and is deleted from the Student table, all
[y=372 x=65 R10.2] her records in the Enrollments and Results tables are instantly wiped out. This prevents
[y=387 x=65 R10.2] "ghost" data from clogging the system.
[y=447 x=212 B10.8] Benefits of Referential Integrity
[y=476 x=63 R10.8] Maintains complete data consistency.
[y=476 x=304 R10.8] Saves time and manual effort for admins.
[y=505 x=63 R10.8] Reduces errors and prevents orphan
[y=521 x=63 R10.8] records.
[y=505 x=304 R10.8] Ensures reliable and accurate reporting.
