[y=54 x=57 B19.2] 4.4 Entity Relationship Model (ER-Model)
[y=97 x=68 B10.8] ★ GOLDEN TOPIC
[y=130 x=57 R12.0] The ER Model is a conceptual tool used to design databases before they are
[y=148 x=57 R12.0] created. It helps designers understand what data needs to be stored and how
[y=166 x=57 R12.0] different pieces are related. It acts like an architect's blueprint for the database.
