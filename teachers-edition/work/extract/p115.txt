[y=419 x=65 B14.4] 6.4.1 Interviews
[y=447 x=65 R10.8] An interview is a planned conversation used to gather information directly from a
[y=463 x=65 R10.8] person. It may be one-to-one or in a small group. Questions are planned in advance,
[y=480 x=65 R10.8] and answers are usually qualitative.
[y=563 x=65 B14.4] 6.4.2 Surveys / Questionnaires
[y=591 x=65 R10.8] A survey is useful when the same set of questions needs to be asked to many people.
[y=607 x=65 R10.8] It may be on paper or created with digital tools like Google Forms or Microsoft Forms.
[y=631 x=64 R10.8] Note: Online survey tools automatically collect responses and display basic charts,
[y=648 x=64 R10.8] making data analysis much easier.
