[y=102 x=65 B14.4] 3. Queries
[y=131 x=65 R10.8] A Query is a request to retrieve specific data from the database. Queries allow you to
[y=147 x=65 R10.8] "ask questions" of your database. For example: "Show me all students who scored
[y=163 x=65 R10.8] above 80%." Queries are incredibly powerful because they filter, sort, and calculate
[y=180 x=65 R10.8] data.
[y=287 x=65 B14.4] 4. Reports
[y=315 x=65 R10.8] A Report is used to present data in a structured, formatted, and visually appealing
[y=331 x=65 R10.8] way, usually for printing or sharing. For example, a school generates a printed report
[y=348 x=65 R10.8] card or a fee summary. You do not enter data into a report; you only read it.

[y=462 x=84 B10.8] Tables: Store the data.
[y=478 x=84 B10.8] Forms: Enter the data.
[y=495 x=84 B10.8] Queries: Search the data.
[y=510 x=84 B10.8] Reports: Print the data.
