[y=54 x=57 B19.2] 5.1.2 Internet of Things (IoT)
[y=97 x=68 B10.8] ★ GOLDEN TOPIC
[y=130 x=57 R12.0] The Internet of Things (IoT) is a system in which physical devices are connected
[y=148 x=57 R12.0] to the Internet and can collect and share data to perform tasks automatically.
[y=240 x=56 R10.8] Example: A smartwatch collects heart rate data and sends it to a mobile phone to
[y=256 x=56 R10.8] display.
[y=288 x=57 B14.4] Components of IoT Systems
[y=318 x=57 R12.0] IoT relies on six foundational components to work properly:
[y=351 x=67 B12.0] 1. Sensors ()
[y=373 x=67 R10.2] Devices that collect information from the physical world. They are the "eyes and ears" of
[y=388 x=67 R10.2] the system.
[y=442 x=82 R10.2] Temperature sensor
[y=458 x=82 R10.2] Motion sensor
[y=473 x=82 R10.2] Fingerprint sensor
[y=488 x=82 R10.2] Smoke / Soil moisture sensor
[y=539 x=67 B12.0] 2. Actuators ()
[y=560 x=67 R10.2] Devices that perform physical actions in response to system commands. They convert
[y=575 x=67 R10.2] digital signals into physical movement.
[y=629 x=82 R10.2] Electric Motor / Switch
[y=645 x=82 R10.2] Automatic door opener
[y=660 x=82 R10.2] Water valve / Fan controller
