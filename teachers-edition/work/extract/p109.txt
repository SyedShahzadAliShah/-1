[y=54 x=57 B19.2] 5.5 Assistive Technologies
[y=97 x=68 B10.8] ★ GOLDEN TOPIC
[y=130 x=57 R12.0] Assistive technologies are devices, software, and systems that help people with
[y=148 x=57 R12.0] disabilities or functional limitations. They allow more people to participate
[y=166 x=57 R12.0] equally in school, work, and society.
[y=267 x=57 B14.4] Categories of Assistive Technologies:
[y=304 x=65 B12.0] 1. Mobility Aids
[y=321 x=65 R10.2] Help people move from one place to
[y=337 x=65 R10.2] another.
[y=360 x=64 R10.2] Examples: Smart wheelchairs, stair lifts.
[y=304 x=313 B12.0] 2. Sensory Aids
[y=321 x=313 R10.2] Support vision and hearing to improve
[y=337 x=313 R10.2] interaction.
[y=360 x=312 R10.2] Examples: Hearing aids, Screen readers
[y=375 x=313 R10.2] (VoiceOver), Braille displays.
[y=426 x=65 B12.0] 3. Communication Aids
[y=444 x=65 R10.2] Assist individuals with speech difficulties
[y=459 x=65 R10.2] to express themselves.
[y=482 x=64 R10.2] Examples: Speech-generating devices,
[y=497 x=65 R10.2] Text-to-speech apps.
[y=426 x=313 B12.0] 4. Cognitive / Learning Aids
[y=444 x=313 R10.2] Help people with memory, reading, and
[y=459 x=313 R10.2] understanding.
[y=482 x=312 R10.2] Examples: Audiobooks, spell-checkers,
[y=497 x=313 R10.2] digital reminder apps.
